🎯
Prep completeness = offer conversion
94% of offered learners had zero prep failures. This single metric separates offered from everyone else more than any other signal in the data.
🗣️
Communication is invisible in early rounds
Communication issues barely appear in R1 data — they surface in R2/R3 where structured explanation and business framing are tested. Cleared-only learners carry 16 flagged instances; offered learners carry 0.
🔧
Ajay Patidar's case = direct curriculum signal
Three separate gaps flagged: NN-based forecasting, OpenAI API usage over library wrappers, RAG + MCP integration. These are teachable and specific — high-priority additions.
📌
Neha Sirohi is incorrectly marked Rejected
Her notes explicitly state: "cleared all rounds, waiting for offer letter to be released but status in the sheet is rejected." This is a data quality issue — she should be in the Offered group.
🧩
Domain mismatch is a placement ops problem
Aditya (analytics vs ML), Sohil Sharma (semiconductor ML), DARRYL DENIS (Snowflake/DBT) — these weren't prep failures. Better role-to-profile matching before placement would have prevented these.
📈
Cleared-only learners try harder, go further
They average more companies (1.5 vs 1.3), more rounds (R2.2 vs R1.9), and nearly double the questions (9.5 vs 5.3). The issue isn't effort or exposure — it's conversion quality at the final stage.
